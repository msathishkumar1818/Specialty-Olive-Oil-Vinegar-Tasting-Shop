The Sommelier Method

How We Taste at the Bar

Authentic extra virgin olive oils reveal their terroir through a disciplined four-stage sensory ritual.

01

Warm the Cobalt Glass

Cup the glass in your palm to warm the oil to 28°C, releasing volatile terpenes and aromatics.

02

Inhale the Orchard Bouquet

Swirl gently and inhale deeply to detect fresh cut grass, green artichoke, and almond blossoms.

03

The Strippaggio Aeration

Slurp briskly with teeth closed to atomize oil droplets across all palate tastebuds.
