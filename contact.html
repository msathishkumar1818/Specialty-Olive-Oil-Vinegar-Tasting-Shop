Cellar Protocol

Tasting Room Etiquette

To preserve delicate bouquets for all patrons, our tasting room follows official IOC sensory standards:

  • Fragrance-Free: Refrain from wearing strong colognes before tasting.
  • Palate Cleansers: Crisp green apples and bread provided between flights.
  • Policy: Service animals welcome; flights recommended for ages 12+.
Head Sommelier on Duty: Marco Bellini • IOC Panel Leader
