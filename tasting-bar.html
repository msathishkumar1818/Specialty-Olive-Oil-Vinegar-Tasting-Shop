Sensory Glassware

Official Cobalt Blue Glass

Engineered specifically for sensory olive oil assessment without visual color bias:

  • Optical Watchglass: Traps volatile aromas until the moment of inhalation.
  • Blind Hue Shield: Conceals oil shade so tasters judge strictly by aroma.
  • Palm Thermal Cup: Base cradles in your palm to warm the oil to 28°C.
Nitrogen Preservation
Tasting Counter Tech

Nitrogen-Sealed Fusti Taps

Eliminates atmospheric oxygen to preserve harvest-day aromas first to final drop:

  • Argon Gas Blanket: Pushes out oxygen so oils never experience decay.
  • 316 Stainless Steel: Food-grade mirror-polished canisters crafted in Italy.
  • 18°C Cellar Tap: Dispensed at constant temperature directly your blue glass.
